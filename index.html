Art Advisory

Approach

MAIA advises collectors on acquiring ancient art. Every piece is researched, its provenance documented, and its place in your home considered.

The mark

Made to be worn

Our mark is drawn from the gold hair rings of the Bronze Age: open, banded, and made to be worn every day.

It is a reminder that each object we handle was once part of someone’s life. We look for pieces that can be lived with again.

M

What guides us

The object leads

We begin with the piece itself: what it is, where it comes from, and when it was made. Everything else follows.

Provenance first

A piece is only as good as its history. We research and document it before any conversation about price.

A place to belong

Ancient art should be lived with, not stored. We consider where a piece will sit, and how it will be seen.

Ancient art, now

The quietest, most powerful thing in the room

An object made two thousand years ago can hold a contemporary space like nothing else. It brings depth, history and calm to a room built yesterday.

MAIA helps you find that object, and helps it belong.

Process

From first conversation to a piece at home

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We begin with you: your home, your taste, and what you would like a collection to say.

  2. 02

    Search

    We look for pieces worth your attention, and bring you only those that meet the brief.

  3. 03

    Due diligence

    Provenance, condition and legal title are researched and documented before any offer is made.

  4. 04

    Acquisition

    We handle the purchase, shipping and insurance, and see the piece safely home.

  5. 05

    Placement

    Where a piece sits matters. We advise on display, light and care, so it is seen as it should be.
